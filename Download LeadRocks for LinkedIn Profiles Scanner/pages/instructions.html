How to use
Export profiles to CSV
  • Make a search https://www.linkedin.com/search/results/people/
  • Hit Export profiles to a CSV
  • Choose how many profiles you want to export
  • Don't leave the page while we prepare your file
User profile
  1. Go to LinkedIn user's profile
  2. Open contact details of this user
Withdraw invitations
  • Go to the Sent invitations page: https://www.linkedin.com/mynetwork/invitation-manager/sent/
  • Hit Withdraw invitations
  • Choose how many
  • Wait till the extension does all the work for you
Invite by job title
  • Create an event
  • When inviting people find this field:
     Select by job title
  • Add one or a few job titles and hit Select
  • All the people with a match will be selected
  • Hit Invite once your invite list is ready
