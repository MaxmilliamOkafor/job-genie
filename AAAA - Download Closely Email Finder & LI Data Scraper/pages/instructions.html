How to use
👥 Export people to CSV
  1. Go to People search https://www.linkedin.com/search/results/people/ or Sales Navigator people serach https://www.linkedin.com/sales/search/people/
  2. Apply any filters
  3. Hit Export
  4. Choose how many records you want to export
  5. Grab a coffee while we download all the people to a CSV. It will be saved to your computer.
👤 User profile
  1. Go to LinkedIn user's profile
  2. Open contact details of this user
📬 Withdraw invitations
  1. Go to the Sent invitations page: https://www.linkedin.com/mynetwork/invitation-manager/sent/
  2. Hit Withdraw invitations
  3. Select the time frame for which to withdraw requests
  4. Wait till the extension does all the work for you
💼 Invite by job title
  1. Create an event
  2. When inviting people find this field:
     Select by job title
  3. Add one or a few job titles and hit Select
  4. All the people with a match will be selected
  5. Hit Invite once your invite list is ready
🔑 Log out
